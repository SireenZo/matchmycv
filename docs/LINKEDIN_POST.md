🚀 I just launched my first public project: **MatchMyCV**, an AI tool that tells you how well your CV matches a job *before* you apply.

As a [Computer Science student at LIU], I kept applying to roles without knowing why I wasn't hearing back. So I built the tool I wished I had.

You paste a job post and your CV (or upload a PDF), and in under a minute you get:
✅ A match score with a breakdown for skills, experience, keywords and education
🎯 The gaps to close, ranked by importance
🔑 The ATS keywords your CV is missing
✍️ Before/after rewrites of your weakest CV bullets
💬 Interview questions aimed at your weak spots

🛠️ Under the hood:
• Next.js 15 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
• Claude AI with structured outputs, so every response follows a typed schema
• Native PDF understanding, with no parsing library
• Guardrails so the AI never invents experience or numbers
